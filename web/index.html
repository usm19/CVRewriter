Who is this?

Each person on this device gets their own space. Everything in a space is encrypted with its owner's passphrase, on the device itself; there are no accounts and no server anywhere.

    Stays unlocked on this device for 90 days, then asks again. If the passphrase is forgotten the space cannot be opened by anyone, including us. On a shared device, do not let the browser save it.

    Sites that block readers

    Civil Service Jobs, NHS Jobs and some others refuse anything that is not a person in a browser. Your browser is already past that, so let it do the reading.

    • Open the job listing and click that bookmark
